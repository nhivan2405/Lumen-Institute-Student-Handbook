# Lumen Institute of Technology — Student Handbook 2026–2027

## 1. About This Handbook
This handbook applies to all undergraduate students from 1 September 2026. Lumen offers two undergraduate programs: the Standard Program and the Honors Program. Rules apply to both programs unless a section says otherwise. If a later section of this handbook, including Section 11 (Amendments), conflicts with an earlier section, the later text applies.

## 2. Programs at a Glance
| Item | Standard Program | Honors Program |
|---|---:|---:|
| Tuition per semester | 42,000,000 VND | 58,000,000 VND |
| Minimum credits per semester | 12 | 15 |
| Maximum credits per semester | 24 | 27 |
| Credits required to graduate | 132 | 140 |
| Minimum GPA to remain in good standing | 2.0 | 3.2 |

## 3. Paying Tuition
Tuition must be paid within 15 days after the semester starts. Late payment adds a one-time fee of 5% of the unpaid amount.

Students may choose the installment plan instead of paying in full. Under the installment plan, tuition is paid in two equal parts: the first within 15 days after the semester starts, and the second by the end of week 8. The installment plan has a fee of 500,000 VND per semester.

## 4. Course Registration and Credit Overload
Each student must register for at least the minimum and at most the maximum number of credits shown in Section 2.

Students with a strong record may register for more than the normal maximum ("credit overload"):
- Standard Program students with a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher may register for up to 27 credits.
- Honors Program students with a cumulative GPA of 3.8 or higher may register for up to 30 credits.

Students on academic probation may not use credit overload (see Section 9).

## 5. Library
The library is open from 7:00 to 21:30, Monday to Saturday, and is closed on Sunday. During exam weeks (weeks 8 and 16 of each semester), the library stays open until 23:00 and also opens on Sunday from 8:00 to 17:00.

Borrowing limits:
- Standard Program students may borrow up to 6 books at a time, for 21 days.
- Honors Program students may borrow up to 10 books at a time, for 28 days.

Books with a red label are reference books and cannot be borrowed.

The late fine is 2,000 VND per book per day. The fine for a single book never exceeds 100,000 VND.

## 6. Scholarships
- Aurora Scholarship: covers 100% of tuition. Requires a GPA of 3.8 or higher and a conduct score of 90 or higher.
- Beacon Scholarship: covers 50% of tuition. Requires a GPA of 3.4 or higher.

The scholarship percentage applies to the tuition of the student's own program. A student can receive only one scholarship per semester. Students on academic probation are not eligible for any scholarship.

## 7. Exam Rules
Students must arrive at least 10 minutes before an exam starts. A student who arrives more than 15 minutes after the start is not allowed to enter the exam room.

A student who misses an exam may request a make-up exam within 3 working days, with supporting evidence. The make-up exam fee is 150,000 VND. If the student missed the exam because of illness and provides a hospital certificate, the make-up exam is free.

## 8. Dormitory
Lumen has two dormitory blocks:
- Block C: 4 students per room, 1,100,000 VND per month.
- Block E: 2 students per room, 1,800,000 VND per month. Honors Program students get priority for Block E.

Curfew in both blocks is 23:00 from Sunday to Thursday. On Friday and Saturday nights, curfew is 24:00.

## 9. Academic Warning and Probation
A student whose semester GPA falls below the minimum for their program (see Section 2) receives an academic warning. A student who falls below the minimum for two semesters in a row is placed on academic probation. Students on probation may register for at most 14 credits per semester.

## 10. Graduation Requirements
To graduate, a student must:
- complete the number of credits required by their program (see Section 2),
- reach English level B2 (equivalent to IELTS 5.5), and
- have a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0.

An Honors Program student who finishes with a cumulative GPA below 3.2 graduates with a Standard degree instead of an Honors degree.

## 11. Amendments (approved November 2026)
The following changes override earlier sections of this handbook:
1. The make-up exam fee in Section 7 is increased to 200,000 VND, effective immediately. The free make-up for illness with a hospital certificate does not change.
2. The library late fine in Section 5 is increased to 3,000 VND per book per day, effective 1 January 2027. The maximum fine of 100,000 VND per book does not change.
3. The installment plan in Section 3 is no longer available to Honors Program students.

## 12. Frequently Asked Questions
**Can I change from the Standard Program to the Honors Program?**
Students may apply to move to the Honors Program after their first year if their cumulative GPA is 3.5 or higher.

**Where do I submit a make-up exam request?**
Submit it to the Academic Affairs Office in Building A, Room 102.

**Who do I contact about scholarships?**
Contact the Student Services Office by email at services@lumen.edu.vn.
